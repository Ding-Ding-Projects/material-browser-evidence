What this set covers

The images were selected from a normally closed run. The UI was inspected in the original PNG bytes, and synthetic records were used. Metadata identifies the exact source pre-dew, built package, renderer, and host. Captures with obscuring notifications were excluded.

Read the evidence method and limits

Evidence is a record of the listed states, not a claim that unlisted workflows passed.
